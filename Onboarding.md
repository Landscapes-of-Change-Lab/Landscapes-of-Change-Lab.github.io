## Onboarding {#onboarding}

Welcome to the Landscapes of Change Lab at UC Santa Barbara!

Here are some resources to help you get settled into the Lab.

The LOC Lab is excited that you have decided to join our team! We hope that these onboarding resources, guidelines, and tips will make your transition to UC Santa Barbara and The Bren School seamless and enjoyable. Most importantly, welcome!

If you are already a student, post-doc, or researcher we hope these resources prove useful as well.

### Office Space

Our physical lab space is in Bren Hall, first floor lab wing, lab room 1310.

Everyone in the lab is provided with desk space; lab members usually sit either in the lab itself, or in their office also located in Bren Hall. LOC Lab meetings are held in the lab room.

### Building & Lab Room Access

Access to Bren Hall requires your UC Santa Barbara ID during non-work hours (8:00am - 5:00pm, Monday - Friday), while offices and lab rooms require a key.

**1. Obtain Lab/Office Keys**: The Bren Administrative Team will issue a key to the lab room and your office space.

**2. Access to Bren Hall**: Once you have purchased your student access card, complete the [Bren School After Hours Building Access Form](https://bren.formstack.com/forms/buildingsecurity) to access Bren Hall after working hours and weekends.

### Borrowing Lab Research Gear

The LOC Lab has shared research gear for field and lab projects; you can check out and return research gear in the [Loc Lab Gear Shed](https://sites.google.com/view/loclabgearshed/home).

**Note: Three simple rules when borrowing gear:**

1.  Use gear lightly (these are expensive items to replace!)
2.  Clean your items so they are ready for the next user and to avoid mold/damage. So, return items without dirt residue, mold, water/wetness, dead batteries etc.
3.  Stay safe out there!

### Parking and Transportation:

Parking at Bren (Lot 1) is staff & faculty only, and requires a parking permit. Parking lot 6, 22 and 50 provide public parking that requires a fee. Full details for campus parking policies and procedures are on the [UCSB parking webpage](https://www.tps.ucsb.edu/parking-ucsb). Note that there is limited short-term/temporary parking available on campus so you may feel it's not necessary to obtain a campus parking pass.
